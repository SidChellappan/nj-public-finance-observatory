Public records, made easier to follow.

Municipal budget records contain valuable information, but reading them means navigating source files, changing field locations, and missing entries. The Observatory brings selected records into a consistent, inspectable format.

Start with a clear question, follow each value back to its source, and keep uncertainty visible. The data, definitions, and rebuild code are available alongside the work.

An example case study.

Evidence you can inspect.

Each published value carries its source sheet, cell, year, and unit. Missing and flagged records stay visible, and unresolved anomalies remain documented.

The Observatory does not rank communities or assign fiscal-health scores. Its purpose is to help readers inspect what the public record reports and understand its limits.
