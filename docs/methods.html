01: Scope

One question, five stable identifiers, eleven years

The unit of observation is one municipality code in one annual NJ DCA Summary sheet. The target codes are Hamilton 1103, Lawrence 1107, Trenton 1111, West Windsor 1113, and Princeton 1114. Records are selected and joined by code; municipality names are never used as join keys.

The expected cross-product is 5 municipalities × 11 budget years = 55 rows. Rows stay in the panel even when every public metric is missing.

02: Source provenance

A pinned state workbook, not hand-copied values

The User-Friendly Budget (UFB) is New Jersey's municipal budget reporting form and database distributed by the Department of Community Affairs. Availability and completeness vary by municipality and budget year. “No UFB Available” means NJ DCA indicates that no UFB is on file; “Significant Data Missing” means a submitted UFB left at least one entire section blank.

The build verifies this checksum before extraction. If the state replaces the file at the same URL, the build stops until the changed workbook is reviewed and provenance is deliberately updated.

The workbook is opened with openpyxl in read-only, data_only=True mode. That reads values distributed in the workbook without executing macros or recalculating external links. It also means cached source values can carry upstream problems; the unreconciled 2024 valuation source-scale anomaly is one example, and its cause has not been established.

03: Source sheets and fields

Header-driven extraction with one documented layout shift

All annual sheets are named 2015 Summary through 2025 Summary. Header row 5 identifies metrics. Rows are searched for the five target codes, so extraction does not depend on a municipality's row position.

Reported net debt is the NJ DCA source measure of gross debt less the deductions recognized in that source for the budget year. Tax collection is the reported percentage of the prior calendar year's levy collected; the latest-valid table therefore shows both the tax reference year and the associated budget year. The source glossary defines per-capita net debt as net debt divided by municipal Census population and total imbalances as the total of identified structural imbalances including offsets.

04: Normalization and missingness

Preserve first; interpret later

  1. Select by code. Read each annual sheet and require exactly one row for every target code.
  2. Keep source evidence. Store the workbook, sheet, row, metric cell, source header, and a text representation of each source value.
  3. Normalize explicit missingness. Convert the exact text “No data” to null. Never turn it into numeric zero. Keep the row.
  4. Convert units visibly. The workbook stores percent-formatted cells as fractions, so public percentage fields multiply the source number by 100.
  5. Do not repair anomalies. Numeric zero remains zero; mismatched per-capita values remain reported values; questionable valuation values remain in audit columns.

The source's two flags are preserved separately. “No UFB Available” means NJ DCA says it has no User-Friendly Budget on file. “Significant Data Missing” means a UFB was submitted but at least one entire section was blank. A blank flag means only that the flag was not asserted; it does not certify completeness.

05: Validation

What stops a build and what produces a warning

Build-stopping errors

  • Duplicate municipality-year keys
  • Any missing target code or row; row count not equal to 55
  • Years outside 2015-2025
  • Negative population or net debt
  • “No data” paired with a normalized number
  • Tax percentages outside 0-100 after conversion
  • A publication-eligible value carrying a source flag or null

Warnings and ledger entries

  • Reported per-capita debt more than 5% from debt ÷ population
  • Consecutive percentage values changing by at least 50×
  • An isolated numeric zero between positive debt years
  • 2024 valuations more than 10× below both adjacent years
  • Required values reported as “No data,” with or without a flag

Automated tests also rebuild the public artifacts from the pinned workbook in a temporary directory and compare their bytes with the checked-in results. A focused formula-mode test verifies that the five anomalous 2024 valuation cells remain external-workbook VLOOKUP formulas tied to the expected municipality codes.

06: Publication rules

Reported values are not the same as verified facts

The chart displays the workbook's reported net debt. Nominal dollars as reported for each budget year; not adjusted for inflation or converted to a common-year dollar basis. Missing, source-flagged, and unreconciled observations become visible gaps; lines are not interpolated across them.

Read each series as a within-municipality record over time. Differences in line height between municipalities are not measures of relative debt burden, fiscal strength, or a better or worse fiscal position.

The latest-valid table chooses the newest value separately for net debt and tax collection. A candidate must be numeric, carry neither missing-data flag, and pass the field's publication rule. Each displayed cell includes its budget year, unit, annual source sheet, and Excel cell.

The 2024 property-valuation cells for all five targets use cached external-workbook VLOOKUP values that are far below 2023 and 2025. The cause has not been established. Those values and the source debt-to-valuation ratios remain in the downloadable audit panel but are excluded from charts, latest-valid tables, and interpretive claims. Reported net debt does not depend on that denominator and remains eligible when its own row passes the rules.

07: Known limitations

What remains unresolved

  • Reported net debt is a statutory, source-defined measure. It is not a complete measure of a local unit's total debt-like obligations, fiscal condition, or credit quality. Obligations not captured by this field may exist; evaluating them would require separately reviewed ACFR disclosures and legal and financial analysis, which this project does not perform.
  • Trenton's 2025 net-debt, per-capita debt, and tax-collection cells say “No data.” Earlier Trenton years also contain unavailable records.
  • Princeton's 2022 source row reports numeric zero for every net-debt component while adjacent years exceed $90 million. Zero is retained, but the chart omits it pending clarification.
  • Several per-capita debt values do not approximately equal the same row's reported debt divided by population. They remain in the audit download but are excluded from charts, summary tables, and interpretive claims.
  • The unreconciled 2024 valuation source-scale anomaly affects all five targets. Its raw values remain available only for audit, and its cause has not been established.
  • The project does not independently audit municipal submissions or establish why a value changed.
  • Nominal dollars as reported for each budget year; not adjusted for inflation or converted to a common-year dollar basis. Definitions or source construction may change over time.

ACS enrichment is excluded because rolling five-year estimates would add overlapping periods and a second set of vintage and uncertainty rules before the base data is stable. EMMA is excluded because issuer matching, disclosure-document extraction, and bond-trading context are separate research problems. Neither source is needed to answer the narrow research question.

08: Corrections

A correction should be reproducible too

A useful correction identifies the municipality code, budget year, field, annual sheet and cell, current Observatory record, exact proposed correction, and supporting public evidence. The reviewer should be able to repeat the check without relying on a screenshot alone.

Open the structured data-correction form. GitHub issues and attachments are public, so do not include confidential, private, or personally identifying information.

Accepted changes will preserve the prior artifact in version history, update the anomaly ledger or extraction rule, add a regression test when appropriate, rebuild all public artifacts, and receive a plain-language changelog entry. Opening an issue does not automatically replace a source-reported value.

Important context

These figures come from self-reported public budget records and have not been independently audited. This project is not a credit rating, investment analysis, or policy recommendation.
